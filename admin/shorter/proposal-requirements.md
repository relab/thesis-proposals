## What is a good proposal for a thesis

*A master thesis is a scientific work.*

 Scientific means, it should ask and answer a research question.
The short time you have for writing a thesis typically will not allow you to invent new things or solve entirely new problems.
A typical research question is of the form:

*How well does methodology/technology X work when applied to problem Y?*

Additionally, in your thesis proposal you should make clear, that your research question is at the frontier of science, how you want to evaluate it, and why it is a plausible approach.

At the **frontier of science** means that the thesis should be related to the scientific state of the art.A typical way to show your thesis is at the frontier of science is, if the method or technology you want to apply and evaluate are recent developments.Optimally, you should cite a recent research paper proposing the method you want to apply.

**Evaluation** should quantify the answer to your research question in your thesis. Note that, to know whether a certain evaluation is good or bad often requires a baseline, which you may have to implement as well. 
For example, to evaluate the research question *Can explicit memory management reduce the memory footprint of a replicated state machine?* you may have to measure and compare the memory footprint of one system implemented with explicit memory management, with one, without explicit memory management (e.g. garbage collected).
In your proposal, you need to explain how you can evaluate your research question and what the baseline should be.

Finally, your proposal should explain, why it is **plausible** that your method may lead to a satisfactory solution for your problem. A thesis may not always produce a positive result and evaluation should be performed with a open expectation. Still, to invest into its evaluation, a positive results should be plausible.
